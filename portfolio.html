About Me

The Editor Behind
the Timeline

Hey, I'm Rakesh Kodathala — the editor behind Life In Frames. I believe every cut should mean something. I don't just assemble footage — I shape emotion, pace, and narrative into content that makes people feel.

My work lives at the intersection of documentary storytelling, cinematic visuals, and modern social content. Whether it's a 5-minute YouTube essay or a 30-second reel, I bring the same level of intentional craft.

I specialize in voiceover-driven narratives, emotional pacing, cinematic color grading, and sound design that elevates every frame. I work primarily in DaVinci Resolve, using its full pipeline — Edit, Color, Fusion, and Fairlight.

How I Work

From Raw Footage
to Final Cut

Step 01

Discovery & Brief

We discuss your vision, audience, and goals. I study your brand, tone, and references. I send a creative brief before I touch the timeline.

Step 02

Rough Cut & Structure

I build the narrative skeleton — story beats, pacing, music selection. You see the shape of the video before any polish goes on.

Step 03

Color, Sound & VFX

Cinematic color grading, sound design, motion graphics, and transitions. This is where the edit transforms from good to unforgettable.

Step 04

Review & Delivery

You review, I refine. Final export optimized for your platform — YouTube 4K, Instagram 9:16, or both. Clean handoff, every time.
